# How Much Does Etsy Take From a $25 Sale?

## The Short Answer

Etsy takes **$2.83 from a $25 sale** in the United States. That is 11.3% of the sale price, before your product cost.

| Fee | How it is calculated | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listing fee | Flat fee per listing | $0.20 |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% of $25 | $1.63 |
| Payment processing fee | 3.0% of $25 + $0.25 | $1.00 |
| **Total Etsy fees** | | **$2.83** |
| **You keep (before product cost)** | $25.00 - $2.83 | **$22.17** |

If the item cost you $8.00 to make or buy, you keep **$14.17**, or 56.7% of the sale price. The rest goes to Etsy fees ($2.83) and your product cost ($8.00).

## The Four Etsy Fees, Explained

Etsy does not take one flat cut. It takes three separate fees on every US sale, plus a fourth for some sellers.

**1. Listing fee: $0.20.** You pay this when you publish a listing, and again each time the item sells and the listing renews. It is the same $0.20 at any price.

**2. Transaction fee: 6.5%.** Etsy takes 6.5% of the total amount the buyer pays for the item, including shipping and gift wrap. On a $25 sale with no shipping charged, that is $25.00 x 6.5% = $1.625, rounded to $1.63.

**3. Payment processing fee: 3.0% + $0.25.** This is what Etsy charges for handling the payment. On a $25 sale, 3.0% is $0.75, plus the $0.25 fixed charge, for a total of $1.00.

**4. Regulatory Operating Fee: 0% for US sellers.** This fee applies only to sellers in certain other countries. US sellers pay nothing here. Non-US sellers pay a percentage that depends on their country, and the rates changed on 22 June 2026. See the non-US section below.

## The $25 Sale, Step by Step

Here is the full calculation for a $25 handmade item sold in the US, with free shipping and no gift wrap:

1. **Listing fee:** $0.20
2. **Transaction fee:** $25.00 x 6.5% = $1.63
3. **Payment processing fee:** ($25.00 x 3.0%) + $0.25 = $0.75 + $0.25 = $1.00
4. **Total fees:** $0.20 + $1.63 + $1.00 = **$2.83**
5. **Deposit to your bank:** $25.00 - $2.83 = **$22.17**
6. **Profit after an $8.00 product cost:** $22.17 - $8.00 = **$14.17 (56.7%)**

The effective fee rate on a $25 sale is 11.3%. That rate is not fixed. It rises on cheaper items and falls on more expensive ones, because the $0.20 listing fee and the $0.25 processing charge stay the same whatever the price.

## $50 and $100 Sales

The same three fees apply at any price. Here is how the numbers scale:

| Sale price | Listing | Transaction (6.5%) | Processing (3.0% + $0.25) | Total fees | You keep (before cost) | Profit with a $20 / $40 cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $25 | $0.20 | $1.63 | $1.00 | $2.83 | $22.17 | $14.17 with an $8 cost |
| $50 | $0.20 | $3.25 | $1.75 | $5.20 | $44.80 | $24.80 with a $20 cost |
| $100 | $0.20 | $6.50 | $3.25 | $9.95 | $90.05 | $50.05 with a $40 cost |

Check the $50 row: $0.20 + $3.25 + $1.75 = $5.20, or 10.4% of the sale. The $100 row totals $9.95, just under 10%. As the price rises, the fixed $0.20 and $0.25 charges matter less, so the effective rate drops toward 9.5%.

The pattern is clear. Low-priced items carry the highest effective fee rate. On a $10 sale, the fees are $0.20 + $0.65 + $0.55 = $1.40, or 14% of the price. On a $5 sale, they are $0.20 + $0.33 + $0.40 = $0.93, or 18.6%. If most of your listings are under $10, bundling items into higher-priced sets is one of the fastest ways to keep more of each sale.

## What Etsy Charges on Shipping

Etsy charges its transaction fee and its payment processing fee on the shipping amount the buyer pays, not just on the item price. The listing fee is flat, so shipping does not change it. The regulatory fee, where it applies, also uses the shipping-inclusive total.

Example: you sell a $30 item and charge $10 for shipping. The buyer pays $40. The fees are calculated on $40:

- Listing fee: $0.20
- Transaction fee: $40.00 x 6.5% = $2.60
- Payment processing fee: ($40.00 x 3.0%) + $0.25 = $1.20 + $0.25 = $1.45
- Total fees: $0.20 + $2.60 + $1.45 = **$4.25**
- You keep: $40.00 - $4.25 = **$35.75**, before the cost of the item and the actual postage you pay.

Free shipping does not remove this cost. If you offer free shipping, the shipping cost sits inside your item price, and Etsy still takes 6.5% plus 3% on the whole amount. Price with the full total in mind.

## Offsite Ads: When Etsy Takes Another 15% or 12%

Offsite Ads is Etsy's advertising program. Etsy shows your listings in ads on Google, Facebook, Instagram, and Pinterest. If a buyer clicks one of those ads and buys from your shop within 30 days, Etsy takes an extra advertising fee on that order.

The rate depends on how much your shop sold in the trailing 12 months:

- **Under $10,000 in trailing 12-month sales:** the Offsite Ads fee is **15%** of the order total, and participation is **optional**. You can opt out in Shop Manager under Settings, then Offsite Ads.
- **$10,000 or more in trailing 12-month sales:** the Offsite Ads fee is **12%** of the order total, and participation is **mandatory for life**. Once your shop crosses $10,000, you stay enrolled even if your sales later drop below $10,000.
- **Cap:** the Offsite Ads fee is capped at **$100 per order**. At 15%, the cap starts to apply on orders over about $667.
- **Attribution window:** 30 days from the ad click.

What this does to a $25 sale: if it came through an Offsite Ad and your shop is under $10,000, the ad fee is $25.00 x 15% = $3.75. Total fees become $2.83 + $3.75 = **$6.58**. After an $8.00 product cost, you keep $25.00 - $6.58 - $8.00 = **$10.42**, instead of $14.17.

On a $100 Offsite Ads sale at 12%, the ad fee is $12.00. Total fees are $9.95 + $12.00 = **$21.95**. After a $40.00 product cost, you keep $100.00 - $21.95 - $40.00 = **$38.05**, instead of $50.05.

Offsite Ads can bring sales you would not have made otherwise, but the fee is large. Check your Etsy stats to see how many of your sales are attributed to Offsite Ads, and whether those orders stay profitable after the extra cut.

## Non-US Sellers: the Regulatory Operating Fee

If you sell on Etsy from outside the US, you also pay a Regulatory Operating Fee, charged on the item price plus shipping plus gift wrap. Etsy changed the whole schedule on 22 June 2026, and the rate depends on your country.

| Seller country | Rate from 22 June 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| United Kingdom | 0.48% |
| France | 1.14% |
| Italy | 0.80% |
| Spain | 0.88% |
| Turkiye | 1.67% |
| Canada | 0.50% |
| Hungary | 1.97% |
| India | 0.05% |
| Vietnam | 1.24% |
| United States | 0.00% (exempt) |

US sellers pay nothing here. Germany and Australia are also exempt at 0%.

Two worked examples show what the extra fee means.

**UK seller, £12 order, no shipping.** Listing £0.20, transaction 6.5% = £0.78, UK payment processing 4.0% + £0.20 = £0.68, regulatory fee 0.48% = £0.06. Total fees: £0.20 + £0.78 + £0.68 + £0.06 = **£1.72**, or 14.3% of the order. Net deposit: £12.00 - £1.72 = **£10.28**.

**France seller, €50 item + €5 shipping.** Listing €0.20, transaction 6.5% of €55 = €3.58, French payment processing 4.0% + €0.30 = €2.50, regulatory fee 1.14% of €55 = €0.63. Total fees: €0.20 + €3.58 + €2.50 + €0.63 = **€6.91**, or 12.6% of the order. Net deposit: €55.00 - €6.91 = **€48.09**.

Payment processing rates also differ by country. The UK pays 4.0% + £0.20, France, Italy, Spain, and Hungary pay 4.0% + €0.30, Turkiye pays 6.5% + 14 TRY, Canada pays 3.0% + CA$0.25, and India pays 5.0% + ₹25.

If your calculator still shows the old regulatory rates, such as UK 0.32% or France 0.47%, it is out of date. Many older calculators also show Spain at 0.40% and Turkiye at 2.07%, which were the original 2021 rates. Always check against Etsy's own help pages.

## How to Keep More of Each Sale

You cannot change Etsy's rates, but you can change the math around them.

**Raise your average order value.** The fixed $0.20 listing fee and $0.25 processing charge punish small orders. A $5 sale loses 18.6% to fees, while a $50 sale loses 10.4%. Bundles and sets push orders into the cheaper range.

**Price gift wrap to cover its own fees.** Etsy charges the 6.5% transaction fee, the processing fee, and the regulatory fee on gift wrap too. On a UK order, the total deduction on a gift wrap add-on is about 11%. A £4.00 gift wrap charge loses about £0.44 to fees before you buy paper and ribbon.

**Watch your Offsite Ads attribution.** If a large share of your orders comes through Offsite Ads, the 15% or 12% fee may be your biggest cost after materials. Shops under $10,000 can opt out, so run the numbers first.

**Recalculate after every rate change.** Etsy changed its regulatory rates in June 2026, and processing rates differ by country. Re-check your margins twice a year against the live rates.

## FAQ

**Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?**
Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee and the 3.0% + $0.25 processing fee are both calculated on the item price plus shipping. On a $30 item with $10 shipping, the fees are $4.25.

**Is Etsy Offsite Ads optional?**
It is optional only if your shop sold under $10,000 in the trailing 12 months. At $10,000 or more, enrolment is mandatory for life at 12% per attributed order, capped at $100.

**Why is the fee percentage higher on cheap items?**
Because the $0.20 listing fee and the $0.25 processing charge are fixed. They take a bigger slice of a $5 sale (18.6% total) than of a $50 sale (10.4% total).

**Do US sellers pay the Regulatory Operating Fee?**
No. US sellers are exempt at 0%. The fee applies to sellers in the UK, France, Italy, Spain, Turkiye, Canada, Hungary, India, and Vietnam at the rates in the table above.

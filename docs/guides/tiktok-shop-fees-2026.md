## The Short Answer

TikTok Shop takes **$2.64 from a $30 sale** in the US when no creator is involved. If a creator drives the sale at a 10% affiliate commission, TikTok takes **$5.64**.

| Fee | How it is calculated | Amount on a $30 sale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Referral fee | 6% of $30 | $1.80 |
| Payment processing fee | 1.8% of $30 + $0.30 | $0.84 |
| Creator affiliate commission | 10% of $30 (only if a creator drives the sale) | $3.00 |
| **Total without affiliate** | $1.80 + $0.84 | **$2.64** |
| **Total with 10% affiliate** | $1.80 + $0.84 + $3.00 | **$5.64** |
| **You keep (before product cost)** | $30.00 - $2.64 / $30.00 - $5.64 | **$27.36 / $24.36** |

There is no setup fee, no monthly fee, and no listing fee on TikTok Shop. You pay only when you sell. The affiliate commission is the variable that changes everything, because you set it yourself.

## The Three Fees, Explained

**1. Referral fee: 6% for most categories.** This is TikTok Shop's main commission, charged on the item price of every completed order. A few categories pay less: selected fine jewelry and pre-owned items pay 5%, and electronics pay 5% in the rate table used by this guide. The referral fee started at its current level on 1 April 2024. There is no separate platform fee on top of it.

**2. Payment processing fee: 1.8% + $0.30.** This covers card and payment handling on each order. On a $30 sale, 1.8% is $0.54, plus the $0.30 fixed charge, for a total of $0.84.

**3. Creator affiliate commission: you set it, usually 10% to 20%.** When a creator makes a video or goes live with your product and a buyer purchases through their link, the creator earns the commission rate you set for that product. TikTok does not set this rate. You do, product by product, and you can set different rates for different products. The typical range sellers use is 10% to 20%. You can set it as low as 1% or as high as 80%, but rates at the extremes either attract no creators or destroy your margin.

## The $30 Sale, Step by Step

Here is the full calculation for a $30 item sold in the US, with no creator involved:

1. **Referral fee:** $30.00 x 6% = **$1.80**
2. **Payment processing fee:** ($30.00 x 1.8%) + $0.30 = $0.54 + $0.30 = **$0.84**
3. **Total fees:** $1.80 + $0.84 = **$2.64**
4. **You keep:** $30.00 - $2.64 = **$27.36**

With a 10% creator affiliate, add $30.00 x 10% = $3.00. Total fees become $2.64 + $3.00 = **$5.64**. You keep $30.00 - $5.64 = **$24.36**.

With a 20% creator affiliate, add $30.00 x 20% = $6.00. Total fees become $2.64 + $6.00 = **$8.64**. You keep $30.00 - $8.64 = **$21.36**.

Now add a $10.00 product cost to see the real profit:

| Affiliate rate | Total fees | Profit after a $10 product cost | Profit margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No affiliate | $2.64 | $17.36 | 57.9% |
| 10% affiliate | $5.64 | $14.36 | 47.9% |
| 20% affiliate | $8.64 | $11.36 | 37.9% |

Check the middle row: $30.00 - $5.64 - $10.00 = $14.36, and $14.36 / $30.00 = 47.9%. Doubling the affiliate rate from 10% to 20% costs you exactly $3.00 more on this order, and cuts your profit by more than a fifth. The affiliate rate is the single biggest lever on your TikTok Shop margin.

## Electronics: the 5% Referral Rate

Electronics pay a 5% referral fee instead of 6%. Here is a $100 electronics sale:

1. **Referral fee:** $100.00 x 5% = **$5.00**
2. **Payment processing fee:** ($100.00 x 1.8%) + $0.30 = $1.80 + $0.30 = **$2.10**
3. **Total fees:** $5.00 + $2.10 = **$7.10**
4. **You keep:** $100.00 - $7.10 = **$92.90**

Compare that with a $100 sale in a general category at 6%: referral $6.00, processing $2.10, total **$8.10**, and you keep **$91.90**. The 1% difference is worth $1.00 on a $100 order, and $10.00 on every $1,000 of electronics sales. Check your exact category rate in Seller Center before you price, because a wrong category assumption is a pricing error on every order.

A $100 general-category sale with a 15% creator affiliate shows how fast the affiliate adds up: referral $6.00 + processing $2.10 + affiliate $15.00 = **$23.10** in total fees. You keep $100.00 - $23.10 = **$76.90** before product cost. The creator takes more than twice what TikTok itself takes on that order.

## TikTok Shop vs Shopify on the Same $25 Product

Sellers often compare TikTok Shop with their own Shopify store. Take a $25 product with a $12.00 product cost:

**TikTok Shop, sold through a creator at 10%:**

- Referral fee: $25.00 x 6% = $1.50
- Processing fee: ($25.00 x 1.8%) + $0.30 = $0.45 + $0.30 = $0.75
- Affiliate commission: $25.00 x 10% = $2.50
- Total fees: $1.50 + $0.75 + $2.50 = **$4.75**
- Profit: $25.00 - $4.75 - $12.00 = **$8.25**

**Shopify, Basic plan with Shopify Payments:**

- Processing fee: $25.00 x 2.9% + $0.30 = $0.73 + $0.30 = **$1.03**
- Profit: $25.00 - $1.03 - $12.00 = **$11.97**

Shopify keeps **$3.72 more** on this order. But Shopify brings no traffic, while the TikTok creator brings the buyer. The $2.50 affiliate commission is the price of that traffic. If the creator's video brings a sale you would not have made on your own store, the $8.25 profit is found money. If the buyer would have found your store anyway, the affiliate fee is money you gave away. That is the real decision: not which platform is cheaper, but whether the creator earns their cut.

## TikTok vs Shopify vs Etsy on a $55 Sale

For a direct three-way comparison, take a $55 sale with a $20.00 product cost and no affiliate or advertising involved:

| Platform | Fees | You keep after a $20 cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shopify (Basic, Shopify Payments) | $1.90 | $33.10 |
| TikTok Shop (no affiliate) | $4.59 | $30.41 |
| Etsy (US) | $5.68 | $29.32 |

Check the TikTok row: referral 6% of $55 = $3.30, processing 1.8% of $55 = $0.99 plus $0.30 = $1.29, total $3.30 + $1.29 = $4.59. You keep $55.00 - $4.59 - $20.00 = $30.41.

Check the Etsy row: listing $0.20 + transaction 6.5% of $55 = $3.58 + processing 3.0% of $55 = $1.65 plus $0.25 = $1.90, total $0.20 + $3.58 + $1.90 = $5.68. You keep $55.00 - $5.68 - $20.00 = $29.32.

TikTok sits in the middle: cheaper than Etsy per order, more expensive than Shopify Payments. But add a 10% affiliate to the TikTok sale and the fees become $4.59 + $5.50 = $10.09, and you keep only $24.91, the lowest of the three. The affiliate rate decides whether TikTok is your cheapest channel or your most expensive one.

## How to Set an Affiliate Rate Without Losing Money

The affiliate rate is your decision, so set it from your margin, not from what other sellers use.

**Start from your target profit, not from 10%.** Work backwards. On a $30 product with a $10 cost, the platform fees without an affiliate are $2.64. If you want at least $12.00 profit per order, the most you can offer a creator is $30.00 - $10.00 - $2.64 - $12.00 = **$5.36**, or about 17%. Offering 20% would leave you $11.36, below your target.

**Use different rates for different products.** A high-margin product can carry a 20% rate and still profit. A low-margin product may only afford 5%. Set the rate per product in Seller Center, and review it when your costs change.

**Test with a low rate first.** A 10% rate still attracts creators if your product converts well and your price is reasonable. Raise it only if you are not getting creator applications. Every extra point of commission comes straight out of your profit on every creator-driven order.

**Watch the refund rule.** When an order is refunded, TikTok returns the referral fee minus a refund administration fee equal to 20% of the referral fee, capped at $5 per item. On a $30 order, the referral fee is $1.80, so the administration fee is $0.36. Small, but it means a refunded order still costs you money.

## FAQ

**How much does TikTok Shop take per sale?**
TikTok takes a 6% referral fee plus 1.8% + $0.30 processing on a standard US order: $2.64 on a $30 sale. If a creator drives the sale, add the affiliate commission you set, usually 10% to 20%. At 10%, the total on a $30 sale is $5.64.

**What is the TikTok Shop referral fee?**
It is TikTok's commission on each completed order: 6% for most categories, 5% for electronics and selected fine jewelry and pre-owned items. It is charged on the item price.

**Do I have to pay a creator affiliate commission?**
No. The affiliate commission applies only to sales a creator drives through their link. Sales from your own shop page or your own videos carry no affiliate commission, only the referral and processing fees.

**Is there a monthly fee for TikTok Shop?**
No. There is no setup fee, no monthly fee, and no listing fee. You pay the referral and processing fees only when an order completes.

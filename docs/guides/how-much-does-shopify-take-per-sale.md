# How Much Does Shopify Take Per Sale?

## The Short Answer

On the Shopify Basic plan with Shopify Payments, Shopify takes **$1.75 from a $50 order** and **$3.20 from a $100 order**. The card processing rate is 2.9% + $0.30 per online order.

| Order value | Processing fee (2.9% + $0.30) | You keep (before product cost) | Profit with a $20 / $40 cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $25 | $1.03 | $23.97 | $11.97 with a $12 cost |
| $50 | $1.75 | $48.25 | $28.25 with a $20 cost |
| $100 | $3.20 | $96.80 | $56.80 with a $40 cost |

That is the per-order cost. On top of it, you pay the monthly plan: **$39 a month** on Basic when billed monthly, or $29 a month when billed yearly. There is no extra Shopify fee on an order when you use Shopify Payments. The 2.9% + $0.30 is the whole per-order charge.

Use the [Shopify Profit Calculator](/tools/shopify-profit-calculator) to run your own numbers with your real product cost and monthly sales volume.

## The Two Costs: Plan and Processing

Shopify charges you in two places, and sellers often mix them up.

**1. The monthly plan.** Basic costs $39 a month billed monthly, or $29 a month billed yearly. The plan pays for your store, hosting, checkout, and admin. You pay it whether you sell one order or one thousand. Grow is $105 a month billed monthly, and Advanced is $399 a month billed monthly. Higher plans lower your card rate, which we cover below.

**2. The card processing fee.** On Basic, Shopify Payments charges 2.9% + $0.30 for a standard online card payment in the US. There is no separate monthly fee for Shopify Payments itself, and no setup fee. The rate drops on higher plans: 2.7% + $0.30 on Grow, and 2.5% + $0.30 on Advanced. Premium cards cost more, about 3.5% + $0.30 on Basic.

Shopify Payments also includes Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and other wallets at the same card rate. If you accept PayPal through Shopify Payments in the US, it is offered as PayPal Wallet at the same checkout.

## The $50 Order, Step by Step

Here is the full calculation for a $50 order on the Basic plan with Shopify Payments:

1. **Card processing fee:** $50.00 x 2.9% = $1.45, plus $0.30 = **$1.75**
2. **Shopify platform fee on the order:** $0.00 (Shopify Payments has no extra per-order fee)
3. **You keep:** $50.00 - $1.75 = **$48.25**
4. **Profit after a $20.00 product cost:** $48.25 - $20.00 = **$28.25 (56.5%)**

The effective processing rate on a $50 order is 3.5%, because the $0.30 fixed charge is a bigger slice of a small order. On a $100 order, the fee is $3.20, which is 3.2% of the order. On a $25 order, the fee is $1.03, which is 4.1% of the order. The fixed 30 cents is why small orders cost more per dollar.

## The $100 Order, Step by Step

1. **Card processing fee:** $100.00 x 2.9% = $2.90, plus $0.30 = **$3.20**
2. **Shopify platform fee on the order:** $0.00
3. **You keep:** $100.00 - $3.20 = **$96.80**
4. **Profit after a $40.00 product cost:** $96.80 - $40.00 = **$56.80 (56.8%)**

## The Monthly Math: What a Month of Sales Really Costs

Per-order fees are only half the picture. The $39 plan fee matters most when sales are low, and fades as volume grows.

Example: you sell 20 orders of $50 in a month, with a $20 product cost on each order.

- **Revenue:** 20 x $50.00 = **$1,000.00**
- **Processing fees:** 20 x $1.75 = **$35.00**
- **Plan fee:** **$39.00**
- **Product cost:** 20 x $20.00 = **$400.00**
- **Profit:** $1,000.00 - $35.00 - $39.00 - $400.00 = **$526.00**

That is a 52.6% profit margin after all Shopify costs and product costs. At this volume, the plan fee ($39.00) costs slightly more than all the processing fees combined ($35.00). If you sold only 5 orders of $50 that month, the plan fee would be $39.00 against $8.75 in processing fees, and your profit would be $250.00 - $8.75 - $39.00 - $100.00 = **$102.25**. Low volume is where the monthly plan hurts most.

## The External Gateway Trap: the Extra 2%

This is the most expensive mistake new Shopify sellers make. If you use a payment provider other than Shopify Payments, such as Stripe as a third-party gateway, Shopify adds a **third-party transaction fee of 2% on the Basic plan**, on top of what the gateway itself charges.

On a $100 order with an external gateway charging 2.9% + $0.30:

- Gateway fee: $100.00 x 2.9% + $0.30 = **$3.20**
- Shopify third-party fee: $100.00 x 2% = **$2.00**
- Total: $3.20 + $2.00 = **$5.20**, instead of $3.20 with Shopify Payments

On a $50 order, the same setup costs $1.75 + $1.00 = **$2.75**, instead of $1.75. Over 100 orders of $50, the extra 2% costs you $100.00 a month for nothing.

The third-party fee drops on higher plans: 1% on Grow, 0.6% on Advanced. But on Basic, the rule is simple. If Shopify Payments is available in your country, use it as your main gateway and you pay no third-party fee at all. Note that in the US, if Shopify Payments is available to you, Stripe is not offered as a separate gateway at all.

## Shopify Payments vs Stripe vs PayPal

A common question is whether Stripe or PayPal is cheaper than Shopify Payments. On a standard US domestic card, Shopify Payments and Stripe cost exactly the same, because Stripe's standard rate is also 2.9% + $0.30. PayPal costs more.

| Payment method | Fee on a $25 order | Fee on a $50 order | Fee on a $100 order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shopify Payments (Basic) | $1.03 | $1.75 | $3.20 |
| Stripe (standard US) | $1.03 | $1.75 | $3.20 |
| PayPal (3.49% + $0.49) | $1.36 | $2.24 | $3.98 |

Check the PayPal numbers: on $100, 3.49% is $3.49, plus $0.49, for $3.98. On $50, 3.49% is $1.75, plus $0.49, for $2.24. On $25, 3.49% is $0.87, plus $0.49, for $1.36.

PayPal costs $0.78 more than Shopify Payments on every $100 order. On 100 orders of $100, that is $78.00 a month extra. PayPal is still worth offering, because many buyers trust it and will not check out without it, but do not make it your only payment method. Use Shopify Payments as the default and offer PayPal alongside it.

One more comparison: on a $100 order, Square charges 2.9% + $0.30 for online payments, the same as Shopify Payments, but Square is not a native Shopify checkout option, so routing through it would also trigger the 2% third-party fee.

## Shopify vs Etsy on a $50 Sale

Sellers often ask whether Etsy or Shopify keeps more of the same sale. On a $50 order with a $20 product cost:

- **Etsy:** listing $0.20 + transaction 6.5% ($3.25) + processing 3.0% + $0.25 ($1.75) = **$5.20 in fees**. You keep $50.00 - $5.20 - $20.00 = **$24.80**.
- **Shopify (Basic, Shopify Payments):** processing $1.75, no other per-order fee. You keep $50.00 - $1.75 - $20.00 = **$28.25**.

Shopify keeps **$3.45 more** in your pocket on this order. But that ignores the $39 monthly plan, which Etsy does not charge, and it ignores Etsy's built-in traffic. Etsy brings buyers to your listings. Shopify brings none, so you pay for traffic yourself through ads or content. The honest comparison is per-order fees (Shopify wins) against traffic cost (Etsy includes it). If you already have your own audience, Shopify is cheaper per order. If you depend on marketplace search, Etsy's higher fee buys you the customers.

## When to Upgrade to Grow

Grow lowers your online card rate from 2.9% to 2.7%, a saving of 0.2% per order, and lowers the third-party fee from 2% to 1%. But Grow costs $105 a month billed monthly, which is $66 more than Basic at $39.

Break-even math: you save $0.10 on each $50 order (0.2% of $50). To cover the extra $66 a month, you need $66.00 / $0.10 = **660 orders of $50 a month**. That is $33,000 in monthly sales. Below that volume, Basic is cheaper even with the higher card rate. Do not upgrade for the rate alone. Upgrade when you need Grow's extra features, such as more staff accounts or lower international rates, not to save 0.2%.

The same logic applies to Advanced at $399 a month. The card rate drops to 2.5%, saving 0.4% per order against Basic, but the plan costs $360 more a month. At $50 average orders, that needs 1,800 orders a month to break even on rate alone.

## FAQ

**How much does Shopify take per sale?**
On the Basic plan with Shopify Payments, Shopify takes 2.9% + $0.30 per online order: $1.75 on a $50 order and $3.20 on a $100 order. There is no extra per-order platform fee. You also pay the $39 monthly plan.

**Is Shopify Payments cheaper than Stripe?**
On a standard US domestic card, they cost the same: 2.9% + $0.30. Stripe is $3.20 on a $100 order, exactly like Shopify Payments on Basic. The difference is that using Stripe as a third-party gateway on Shopify adds Shopify's 2% third-party fee, making it $5.20 on a $100 order.

**What is the 2% Shopify fee?**
It is the third-party transaction fee. If you use any payment gateway other than Shopify Payments, Shopify adds 2% of the order value on the Basic plan, 1% on Grow, and 0.6% on Advanced. It applies on top of the gateway's own fees.

**Does Shopify charge a fee on every sale even with Shopify Payments?**
Shopify Payments itself charges the card processing fee (2.9% + $0.30 on Basic), but Shopify adds no separate per-order platform fee. The only other Shopify cost is your monthly plan.
